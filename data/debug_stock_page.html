PENTAMASTER CORPORATION BERHAD KLSE Summary
Pentamaster Corporation Berhad is a Malaysia-based automation manufacturing and technology solutions provider. Its segments include Automated test equipment (ATE), Factory automation solutions (FAS), and Smart control solution systems. The ATE segment is engaged in design, development, and manufacturing of standard and nonstandard automated equipment. The FAS segment is engaged in design, development and installation of integrated factory automation solutions. The smart control solution system is engaged in project management, smart building solutions and trading of materials. Its solutions include Discrete and Integrated Circuits, Electro-Optical/MEMS Sensors, solutions for silicon carbide (SiC), gallium nitride (GaN) Power Modules, Factory Automation, Warehouse Automation Solutions, Micro AI Automation and Machine and Product Contract Manufacturing. Its solutions also include automated assembly solutions, automated optical inspection solutions, burn-in solutions and test solutions.
