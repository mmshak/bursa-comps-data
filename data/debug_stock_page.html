CIMB GROUP HOLDINGS BERHAD KLSE Summary
CIMB Group Holdings Berhad is engaged in banking and financial services. The Company offers consumer banking, commercial banking, wholesale banking, transaction banking, Islamic banking and asset management products and services. Its segments include Consumer Banking, Commercial Banking, Wholesale Banking, and CIMB Digital Assets & Group Funding. Consumer Banking provides everyday banking solutions to individual customers covering both conventional and Islamic financial products and services such as residential property loans, and non-residential property loans. Commercial Banking offers products and services for customer segments comprising small and medium-scale enterprises and mid-sized corporations. Their products and services include banking credit facilities, trade financing, cash management, and an online business banking platform. Wholesale Banking comprises investment banking, corporate banking, treasury and markets, transaction banking, equities and private banking.
