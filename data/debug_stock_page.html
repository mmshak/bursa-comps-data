MANULIFE HOLDINGS BERHAD KLSE Summary
Manulife Holdings Berhad is a Malaysia-based investment holding company. The Company offers a range of financial protection, health and wealth management products and services to meet different customer needs. Its segments include Investment holding, Life insurance and Asset management services. The Investment holding segment includes investment holding operations and other segments, including trust funds over which the Company has significant control. The Life insurance segment underwrites participating life and non-participating life insurance and unit-linked products. The Asset management services segment includes asset management, unit trust and private retirement scheme funds. The Company's product categories include life insurance, health, save, invest, SME business, signature and bancassurance. Its life insurance products include term life and whole life. Its life insurance plans cover against disabilities or loss of lives, whether it is due to natural causes or accidental.
