WILLOWGLEN MSC BERHAD KLSE Summary
Willowglen MSC Berhad is a Malaysia-based company providing technological solutions for a range of infrastructure, businesses, residential, commercial, and industrial needs. The Group operates in two principal geographical areas for the sales, implementation and maintenance of computer-based control systems. Its segments include Malaysia, Singapore, and Others. The Others segment is engaged in investment holdings. Its products and services include Integrated Control and Supervisory System (ISCS), Security Screening System (SSS), Integrated Monitoring System (IMS), Intelligent Transport System (ITS), Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT), and Electric Vehicle Charger (EVC), and Energy Storage System (ESS). ISCS enables the monitoring and command control of multiple subsystems under a single unified system. SSS is a comprehensive solution designed to elevate security measures across diverse environments. IMS sends real-time alerts and information through a comprehensive set of terminals.
