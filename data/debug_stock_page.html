EDARAN BHD KLSE Summary
Edaran Berhad is a Malaysia-based company that is engaged in the provision of management services and investment holding. Its segments include Information technology and services, Telecommunications, Investment holding and Lifestyle. The Information technology and services segment is engaged in provisioning, installation, commissioning, integration and maintenance of information technology products and related services and provisioning of technology for the smart technology industry and for the integrated data center. The Telecommunications segment is engaged in the provisioning, installation, commissioning and maintenance of power supply equipment for telecommunication systems and integration and maintenance of telecommunication equipment and related services. The Lifestyle segment is involved in lifestyle activities, special interest projects and distribution of frozen halal meats. Its subsidiaries include Edaran IT Services Sdn. Bhd., Edaran Lifestyle Sdn. Bhd., and others.
