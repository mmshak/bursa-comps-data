AIMAX BERHAD KLSE Summary
Hong Seng Consolidated Berhad is an investment holding company. The Company, through its subsidiaries, is engaged in gloves manufacturing and trading, financial services, and healthcare. It provides diagnostics and health screening laboratory services, and wholesale of pharmaceutical, medical, and healthcare products via a digital healthcare platform. The segments include Healthcare, Financial Services, Gloves, Search and Advertising, and Others. The Healthcare segment covers marketplace, medical diagnostic and research laboratory, and wholesale of pharmaceutical and medical goods. The Financial Services segment offers moneylending. The Gloves segment involves the manufacturing and trading of rubber gloves. The Search and Advertising segment develops online presence and advertising solutions, operates a search platform, publishes business directory journals, and provides content development and database marketing. The Others segment includes investment holding and dormant companies.
