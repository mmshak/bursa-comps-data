INFOLINE TEC GROUP BERHAD KLSE Summary
Infoline Tec Group Berhad is an information technology (IT) infrastructure and cybersecurity provider. It offers holistic IT solutions ranging from server solutions, storage solutions, and networking solutions. Its segments are IT infrastructure solutions, cybersecurity solutions, managed IT services and other IT services, and trading of ancillary hardware and software. IT Infrastructure Solutions segment is engaged in the design and implementation of IT infrastructure solutions, which include assessing and evaluating customers’ business environments to consult and recommend an appropriate IT infrastructure design that meets their business needs. The Cybersecurity Solutions segment offers a combination of hardware and software products as well as installation and implementation services for the prevention of cyber threats and attacks. Managed IT Services and Other IT Services segment offers managed network services, such as outsourced monitoring and management of customers’ network.
