AWANBIRU TECHNOLOGY BERHAD KLSE Summary
AwanBiru Technology Berhad is a Malaysia-based investment holding company, which provides multi-cloud management services. The Company's segments include Software and services, Talent, Concession, and Others. The Software and services segment provides license distribution, implementation, system integration, managed services, application development, other cloud related solutions, and services. The Talent segment provides provision of career placement, talent upskilling, reskilling, training, and certification. The Concession segment is engaged in the delivery of total solution for the integrated and comprehensive core immigration system that includes design, customize, install, configure, test, commission, and maintenance throughout the concession period. The Others segment engaged in the investment holding services. The Company’s service offerings include Infrastructure Modernization and Cloud Migration, Work Transformation, Smart Analytics, and Professional Services.
