IHH HEALTHCARE BERHAD KLSE Summary
IHH Healthcare Berhad is a Malaysia-based integrated healthcare provider. The Company operates a network of over 80 hospitals across approximately 10 countries. The Company’s business segments include Hospital and Healthcare, Labs, PLife REIT, and Others. The Company’s Hospital and Healthcare segment operates in Singapore, Malaysia, India, Greater China, Turkey, Europe, and Southeast Asia. The Labs segment provides clinical, pathology, and genetics diagnostic laboratory services. The PLife REIT segment functions as a real estate investment trust. The Company’s subsidiaries include Integrated Healthcare Holdings Limited, Integrated Healthcare Turkey Yatirimlari Limited, IHH Financial Services Pte. Ltd., and IHH Laboratories Holdings Sdn. Bhd.
