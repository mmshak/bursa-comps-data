JF TECHNOLOGY BERHAD KLSE Summary
JF Technology Berhad is a Malaysia-based innovator and manufacturer of test contacting solutions for global integrated circuit (IC) makers. The Company's business is focused on the manufacturing and trading of electronic products, components and test probes, including production, packaging, marketing and distribution of its products, principally in Malaysia. The Company's product categories include radio frequency (RF) & precision analog, automotive/high power, customized test solutions and manual actuators. The Company's products include EZ, GAMMA, OSTRICON, ACMA, ALPHA, ZIGMA, PHANTOM, THOR, and various others. Its solutions range from productivity improvement, increase in parallelism, tester and/or handler platform migration, to full turnkey test solution development for new silicon. The Company also specializes in probe card manufacturing. Its subsidiaries include J Foong Technologies Sdn. Bhd., JF Microtechnology Sdn. Bhd., JF International Sdn. Bhd., and JF Testsense Sdn. Bhd.
