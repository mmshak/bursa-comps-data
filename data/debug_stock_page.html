SUPERMAX CORPORATION BHD KLSE Summary
Supermax Corporation Berhad is an investment holding company, engaged in the production of rubber gloves. The Company is an international manufacturer, distributor and marketer of medical devices, namely medical gloves and contact lenses. It operates through four segments: Investment holding, Manufacturing of gloves, Trading of gloves and Others. It produces over 23 billion pieces of gloves in both Natural Rubber Latex Gloves and Nitrile Synthetic Latex Gloves. The Company owns range of brands, such as Supermax, Aurelia, and Maxter, which are used by hospitals, dental practices, home healthcare, laboratories, food services, tattoos and beauty industries. It distributes its gloves through distribution centers set up around the world as well as in collaboration with a wide network of distributors. Its subsidiaries include Supermax Property Holdings Sdn Bhd, Supermax Glove Manufacturing Sdn Bhd, Maxter Glove Manufacturing Sdn Bhd, Supergamma Sdn. Bhd., and others.
