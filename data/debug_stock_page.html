AEON CREDIT SERVICE (M) BERHAD KLSE Summary
AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad is a Malaysia-based company. The Company is principally engaged in the provision of payment schemes, personal financing schemes, issuance of payment cards under the international brand names of Visa and MasterCard, and money lending service. Its products and services include Product Financing, Small and medium enterprises (SME) Financing, Personal Financing, Payment Business, Insurance Brokerage Business, and Islamic Digital Banking. Its Product Financing includes motorcycle financing, auto financing, and objective financing. Its Payment Business includes classic card, gold card, platinum card, AEON Member Plus Visa Card, privilege card for hawkers and petty traders, and AEON wallet and virtual card. Its Insurance Brokerage Business includes conventional and takaful insurance products including life insurance are distributed by AEON Insurance Brokers (M) Sdn. Bhd. Its Personal Financing includes AEON i-cash based on unsecured shariah compliant.
