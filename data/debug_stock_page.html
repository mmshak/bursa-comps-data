GLOBETRONICS TECHNOLOGY BERHAD KLSE Summary
Globetronics Technology Bhd. is an investment holding company. The Company is principally involved in the manufacture, assembly, testing and sale of integrated circuits, optoelectronic products, small outline components, light-emitting-diode (LED) components and modules, sensors and optical products, electronics/semiconductor components and technical plating services for the semiconductor and electronics industries. It provides various products and services, such as semiconductor; LED, encoder and sensor; chemical and IDM supplier, and information technology solutions and services. Its manufacturing activities are performed in Malaysia, and its sales and distribution activities are performed in Malaysia, Singapore, and United States of America. Its subsidiaries include Globetronics Sdn. Bhd., Globetronics Manufacturing Sdn. Bhd., and ISO Technology Sdn. Bhd., among others. Globetronics Sdn. Bhd. is engaged in wafer level die process of integrated circuits and optoelectronic products.
