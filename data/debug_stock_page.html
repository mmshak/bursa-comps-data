ELSOFT RESEARCH BERHAD KLSE Summary
Elsoft Research Berhad is a Malaysia-based company, which is principally involved in research, design, development, and manufacture of automated test equipment (ATE), automation and industrial equipment, burn-in systems, and application specific embedded control systems for semiconductor and optoelectronic industries. It operates through three geographical segments: Malaysia, Asia, and the United States. The Company’s products include Industrial Input/output (IO) Board, automated Light-emitting diode (LED) test equipment, LED burn-in systems, solar cell testers and LED testers. The Company’s primary markets are in the automotive, smart devices and general lighting industries. The Company’s subsidiaries include Xyrius Solutions Sdn. Bhd, which is focused on machinery, automation and industrial equipment, and Elsoft Systems Sdn. Bhd. (ESSB), which is engaged in designing and production of test, burn-in and embedded test equipment and its related modules for the electronics industry.
