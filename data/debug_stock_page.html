HARTALEGA HOLDINGS BERHAD KLSE Summary
Hartalega Holdings Berhad is an investment holding company. The Company is engaged in the manufacture and sale of nitrile and latex gloves. Its product categories include market segment, glove type, speciality gloves, and smart packing. Its market segment includes healthcare, dental, life sciences, food, industrial, spa and beauty salon, veterinary, and tattoo. Its glove type includes nitrile, latex, polyisoprene, and polychloroprene. Its speciality gloves include antimicrobial, colloidal oatmeal coated, accelerator free, biodegradable, and polychloroprene glove. Its smart packing includes Goodpac and Goodpac +. Its subsidiaries include Hartalega Sdn. Bhd., Hartalega NGC Sdn. Bhd., Hartalega Research Sdn. Bhd., MUN Global Sdn. Bhd., Hartalega NSM Sdn. Bhd., Hartalega Corporate Services Sdn. Bhd., among others. Hartalega Sdn. Bhd. and Hartalega NGC Sdn. Bhd. is engaged in manufacturing of latex gloves. Hartalega Research Sdn. Bhd. is engaged in research and development.
