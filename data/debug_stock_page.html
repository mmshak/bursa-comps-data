VITROX CORPORATION BERHAD KLSE Summary
ViTrox Corporation Berhad is a Malaysia-based investment holding company, which is focused on development of three-dimensional (3D) and line scan vision inspection systems. It is engaged in the development and production of vision inspection systems and printed circuit board assemblies for microprocessor applications. It offers its products and services through its core business units: Machine Vision System - Standard (MVS-S), Machine Vision System - Tray-based (MVS-T), Automated Board Inspection (ABI), Integrated Industrial Embedded Solutions (IIES), and Agritech. Its solutions include back-end semiconductors, SMT PCB assembly, integrated industrial embedded solutions and industry 4.0. It serves customers from semiconductor outsourced assembly and test companies, printed circuit board manufacturers, electronics assemblies companies, original equipment manufacturers, original design manufacturers, electronics manufacturing services providers and contract manufacturers around the world.
