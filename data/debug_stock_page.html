ADVENTA BHD KLSE Summary
Adventa Berhad is a Malaysia-based company, which is engaged in investment holding and provision of management services. The Company's segments include Healthcare and Corporate. The healthcare business segment comprises the supply of healthcare and related products and services to hospitals, healthcare centers and pharmacies. The Company's subsidiaries include Adventa Healthcare Sdn. Bhd., Nextech Med Pte. Ltd., PT Adventa Biotech International (PT Adventa Biotech), PTM Progress Trading & Marketing Sdn. Bhd., and others. Adventa Healthcare Sdn. Bhd. is engaged in the distribution of medical and healthcare equipment, appliances, and medical disposal products. Nextech Med Pte. Ltd. is an investment holding company. PTM Progress Trading & Marketing Sdn. Bhd. is engaged in provision of storage and warehousing services.
