JOHAN HOLDINGS BERHAD KLSE Summary
Johan Holdings Berhad is a Malaysia-based company that is engaged in investments holding and management business. The Company is engaged in the manufacture, sale and distribution of gloves, property development, resorts and hotels. The Company's segments include General Trading, Property, Hospitality and card services, Investment holding and secretarial services, and Healthcare. Its subsidiaries include William Jacks & Company (Malaysia) Sdn. Bhd., which is engaged in investment holding, provision of data processing services and trading of engineering and building material; Dynacare Sdn. Bhd., which is engaged in the manufacturing and dealing of rubber gloves and medical personal protection equipment, and Johan Management Services Sdn. Bhd. engaged in the provision of secretarial and management services and an insurance agent. Its other subsidiaries include Johan Land Sdn. Bhd., Johan Properties Sdn. Bhd., Johan Equities Sdn. Bhd. and Johan Pasifik Sdn. Bhd.
