THETA EDGE BERHAD KLSE Summary
Theta Edge Berhad is a Malaysia-based investment holding company. The Company is an information communication and technology (ICT) service provider with a focus on information technology solutions and system integration, telecommunication engineering services and civil works, and green energy and environmental infrastructure consultancy services. Its segments include Information Technology and Telecommunication services (Telco). The Information Technology segment consists of system integration work, trading of hardware and software. The Telco segment is engaged in the provision of telecommunication engineering and communication services. Its technology services include custom applications development, cloud and data center modernization, managed services and data center in a box, network services, smart solutions and ESG, fintech solutions, healthcare as a service, and device as a service. Its subsidiaries include Advanced Business Solutions (M) Sdn. Bhd. and Impianas Sdn. Bhd.
