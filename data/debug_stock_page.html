CUSCAPI BERHAD KLSE Summary
Cuscapi Berhad is a Malaysia-based company engaged in the business of information technology (IT) services. It is involved in the provision of restaurant management solutions and offers a range of integrated solutions for the industry, including but not limited to the point of sales (POS) solutions, outlet management solutions, information technology security solutions, IT consulting services and contact center outsourcing services. Its products include C360Engage, Transight, and others. C360Engage is an integrated POS platform that runs on a hybrid cloud, allowing real-time access to restaurant data anytime and anywhere. Transight is an on-premises comprehensive suite of food and beverages (F&B) business management solutions. Its segments include sale of POS, which is involved in the sale of software, hardware, maintenance services and other support services for point-of-sales to all customers; trading of digital assets, which is involved in trading of digital assets, and corporate.
